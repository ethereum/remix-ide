# Deploy a Base Mini App

The dApps generated by QuickDApp can also be deployed as Base mini apps. Deploying your dApp as a Base mini app allows you to embed it in Base App, making it instantly accessible to users without requiring them to visit an external site.

## Prerequisites

Before you can deploy your dApp as a Base mini app, you need the following:

- A smart contract compiled and deployed using the Deploy & Run plugin.
- A UI for your contract generated by {doc}`QuickDApp </quickdapp>`.
- A browser wallet such as MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet, to own the ENS subdomain. On Remix Desktop, select **Browser Wallet** in the Deploy & Run environment dropdown and connect it.

```{important}
When generating your dApp UI in QuickDApp, you **must** expand "**More options**" and check the "**Create as a Base mini-app**" box before clicking "**Generate DApp**". Skipping this step will produce a standard dApp that cannot be registered as a mini app.
```

## Deploying as a Base mini app

On the preview page of the generated UI, you will find the Base mini app **Setup Wizard**. It has three steps: **Config**, **Deploy**, and **Verify**.

![Base mini app setup wizard in the QuickDApp preview page](images/mini-app/setup-wizard.png)

The **Config** step requires the Base mini-app ID meta tag of an app, in the form `<meta name="base:app_id" content="..." />`. To get the meta tag of an app, you have to create one on [Base.dev](https://base.dev/).

### Creating a Base app

To create a Base app, navigate to [base.dev](https://base.dev/) and log in. If you don't have any existing app you will be prompted to create your first app. If you have existing apps, you will be redirected to the dashboard of your latest app.

On the dashboard, click the app dropdown, and select "**Register new app**"

![Register new app option in Base.dev app dropdown](images/mini-app/create-new-app.png)

On the next page, name the app, and click "**Continue**".

![App name entry on Base.dev](images/mini-app/name-screen.png)

This will take you to the "Add Domain" page.

![Base App ID meta tag on Base.dev](images/mini-app/mini-app-tags.png)

On this page, copy the meta tags, paste it in the setup wizard, and click "**Save & Next**".

```{note}
The App URL should be blank in this step, you will deploy and get the ENS domain in the next step.
```

### Registering an ENS domain

In the **Deploy** step, you will register an ENS subdomain for free under `remixdapp.eth`. Choose your preferred name, for example, `flashloan` -> `flashloan.remixdapp.eth` and click "**Deploy & Next**". A confirmation window opens with the name you chose; review it and click "**Register**".

Before you register, choose your subdomain name carefully. Names are unique and first-come, first-served. Once registered, the name is permanently tied to your browser wallet address (MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, etc.) and cannot be claimed or overwritten by any other wallet. If you already own the name, re-registering it will permanently replace the previous deployment.

For example, if you registered a voting dApp at `myvote.remixdapp.eth` and later register a token swap dApp under the same name, `myvote.remixdapp.eth` will resolve to the token swap dApp and the voting dApp will no longer be accessible at that address.

## Verifying ownership of the mini app

In the **Verify** step, you will see your dApp's deployed URL ending in `.limo`.

```{note}
`.limo` is a gateway service that resolves ENS domains to websites.
```

![Deployed dApp URL ending in .limo in the setup wizard](images/mini-app/ens-name.png)

Copy the URL, then go to your app's settings on Base.dev and open "**Verify & Add URL**". Paste the URL in the "**App URL**" field and click "**Verify & Add**" to confirm ownership.

Go back to Remix and click the "**I've Verified on Base.dev**" button. Your dApp is now accessible on the Base app.

![Successfully deployed dApp accessible on the Base app](images/mini-app/success-screen.png)

## Managing your live mini app

After you verify, the Setup Wizard becomes a dashboard for your live app. From here you can:

- **Publish changes**: Edit your dApp's files in the File Explorer, or use **Ask AI to Update**, then click "**Publish Changes**" to redeploy it to IPFS and update the ENS record.
- **Share** your app's URL.
- See previous deployments under **Deployment History**.

To move the app to a different subdomain, click "**Re-configure Settings**", go to the **Deploy** step, and click "**Change**" next to the domain. You will then have to redeploy the app and verify the new URL on Base.dev again.
